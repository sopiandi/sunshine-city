# Sunshine City v3

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/sopiandi/sunshine-city/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/c/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/sopiandi/sunshine-city/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/c/gta6-01)

An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the
[make.fun gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/c/gta6-01). You are **Kai Reyes**,
a wheelman in the original coastal city of Sunshine City: steal cars, outrun
the Sunshine PD, run five story missions across Marina Bay and the Old Docks,
and stay alive long enough to get paid.

Play it: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or run it locally:

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

**Controls** — WASD / arrows to walk and drive, Shift to run, E to enter or
steal a car, Space or click to shoot, R to reload, P to pause, M to mute,
N for a new game, Q to quit to the menu. On touch devices: a virtual
joystick on the left and SHOOT / CAR / RELOAD / PAUSE buttons on the right.

**Features** — a living city with traffic that queues and yields, pedestrians
who cross, pause and flee, a wanted level (0–5) with chasing police cars and
roadblocks, five story missions with live distance guidance, an edge arrow
and minimap, full save / continue (position, health, money, mission, ammo,
wanted and your car's colour), procedural music and sound effects, and
visible car damage with smoke and stalls.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no libraries, no network
requests, no build step.
